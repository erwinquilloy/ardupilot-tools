The wing part is simulated at the ground speed you set, chasing the QRTL target at no more than the sink rate in the Estimate box. Airbrake holds altitude (confirmed in the 10-05 log: 24.6 → 23.4 m). The VTOL descent uses the landing speed curve from landing_descent_rate_ms(). The turn back toward home is not modelled.

How QRTL picks the target height

Inside the start point, QRTL follows a fixed glide line toward home. Every number in it comes from your parameters.

  • Radius is the larger of |WP_LOITER_RAD| and |RTL_RADIUS|. Inside 2 × radius the target is Q_RTL_ALT itself.
  • Slope is 0.6 × TECS_SINK_MAX ÷ AIRSPEED_CRUISE: the code plans the glide at 60 % of the maximum sink rate.
  • RTL_ALTITUDE −1 means the height you were at when QRTL started (v2.0 fix a9cf161). A positive value is the cap, so selecting QRTL below it from far out makes the plane climb first.
  • Airbrake starts at the stopping distance plus 2 s of travel. With a tailwind it starts further out (log: 187–207 m at 26–27 m/s).

Rule of thumb: to reach Q_RTL_ALT on the wing, select QRTL at least from home.

General formula for any VTOL plane

Direct QRTL, ArduPlane 4.7 code. Every quadplane runs the same logic; only the parameter values differ.

1. Radius and height to lose

R = max(|WP_LOITER_RAD|, |RTL_RADIUS|) H_ret = RTL_ALTITUDE (entry height if −1, fork v2.0) ΔH = max(0, H_ret − Q_RTL_ALT)

2. Where the glide starts

D_start = 2R + AIRSPEED_CRUISE × ΔH ÷ max(0.6 × TECS_SINK_MAX, 1) limited to 2R … 20R

3. Target height at distance d

d ≥ D_start → H_ret d ≤ 2R → Q_RTL_ALT in between → Q_RTL_ALT + ΔH × (d − 2R) ÷ (D_start − 2R) slope (no cap) = 0.6 × TECS_SINK_MAX ÷ AIRSPEED_CRUISE m per m

4. Glide only happens far enough out

d_entry ≥ 1.5 × AIRSPEED_CRUISE² ÷ (2 × Q_TRANS_DECEL)

Closer than this, QRTL goes straight to airbrake at its current height.

5. Airbrake point

d_airbrake = gs² ÷ (2 × Q_TRANS_DECEL) + 2 × gs (gs = ground speed)

6. Will it reach Q_RTL_ALT?

required sink ≈ 0.6 × TECS_SINK_MAX × gs ÷ AIRSPEED_CRUISE

A tailwind raises it. If the plane can't hold that sink, or QRTL is selected inside D_start, it arrives above Q_RTL_ALT and airbrake holds that height over home.

Version caveat: with RTL_ALTITUDE −1, upstream ArduPlane 4.6.3 and 4.7.1 use ΔH = 0 and dive to Q_RTL_ALT as soon as QRTL is selected. The fork's v2.0 fix (a9cf161) uses the entry height instead.
