ArduPilot Tools

Planning and tuning notes for ArduCustom, the ArduPlane fork. Everything runs in your browser; files you load stay on your computer.

QRTL Planner Where a QRTL starts its glide, how low it gets on the wing, and how long it hovers. Load a .param file or a .bin log to use your own values and compare against what the plane flew. ArduCustom v2.0 · ArduPlane 4.7.1 LAND_WIND_BIAS Explorer Which DO_LAND_START the plane picks for an autoland, and why. Move the plane, turn the wind and set LAND_WIND_BIAS, LAND_WIND_DIST and LAND_WIND_STRICT to see the scores and the GCS messages. ArduCustom v1.0 and later · full and light Adaptive Pitch Trim Explorer Edit the PTCH_TRIM_SPD / PTCH_TRIM_OFS curve and see the trim it adds at each airspeed, including the fade near the stall. Load a .param file for your values, or a .bin log to fit the curve from level flight the same way the in-flight learn does. ArduCustom v2.1 · ArduPlane 4.7.2 · full and light Bitmask Calculator Tick the bits you want for any bitmask parameter, such as FLIGHT_OPTIONS, Q_OPTIONS or ARMING_SKIPCHK, and get the value to enter. Reads the fork's own parameter definitions, so the fork-only bits are listed too. ArduCustom v2.0 · v1.1 definitions selectable Full Parameter List All 6,482 parameters in the v2.0 firmware with their descriptions, ranges, units and values, searchable by name or text. Includes the fork's own parameters. ArduCustom v2.0 · ArduPlane 4.7.1 · full and light TECS Tuning Helper (Ethos / EdgeTX) A Lua script for your radio that walks you through TECS tuning in flight, step by step, from live telemetry, then shows the finished values and saves them to a log on the radio. No laptop at the field. Needs passthrough telemetry over CRSF (Crossfire, ELRS) or FrSky SPort. GitHub repo · FrSky Ethos and EdgeTX radios
