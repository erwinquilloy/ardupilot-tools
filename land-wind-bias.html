ArduPlane Custom · fork feature · landing sequence choice

LAND_WIND_BIAS Explorer

When the plane needs an autoland (RTL autoland, battery failsafe, fence breach, or a DO_LAND_START from the GCS), upstream ArduPlane flies to the nearest DO_LAND_START. With LAND_WIND_BIAS above 0, the fork also weighs which landing faces into the wind. Drag the plane, turn the wind and move the sliders to see which landing it picks and why.

Click or drag on the map to move the plane. Each arrow is a landing approach, drawn from its DO_LAND_START point toward the next waypoint. Grid squares are 500 m. Example mission, not your real one.

Below 1 m/s the estimate is not trusted and the nearest landing is used.
0 = upstream (nearest only). 1 = a direct tailwind counts as twice as far.
0 = no cap. Otherwise only DO_LAND_STARTs within this distance of the plane are considered.

Scores

DO_LAND_STARTApproachDistancecos(approach − wind from)MultiplierScoreStatus

Lowest score wins. Multiplier = 1 − LAND_WIND_BIAS × cos. A full headwind (cos +1) shrinks the distance, a full tailwind (cos −1) stretches it.

Worked example: no landing faces into the wind

Wind from 87° at 6 m/s, plane 300 m north and 600 m east of home, LAND_WIND_BIAS 0.50, no cap. North and South field are crosswind (93° off), East strip is a straight tailwind. Press No headwind landing to load it.

The wind only stretches or shrinks a landing's distance; it never rules one out. A full tailwind at most multiplies the distance by (1 + BIAS), so East strip still wins by being about 260 m closer. From LAND_WIND_BIAS 0.55 up, North field (crosswind) wins instead. Even at 1.0, a tailwind landing less than half as far as every other one is still picked. LAND_WIND_DIST and LAND_WIND_STRICT only filter by distance, so they can't stop it either. To be sure of an into-wind choice, give each field approaches in both directions (for example 09 and 27).

How the fork decides

  1. If LAND_WIND_BIAS is 0, use upstream: nearest DO_LAND_START.
  2. If the wind estimate is under 1 m/s, use upstream: nearest.
  3. If LAND_WIND_DIST is set, drop every DO_LAND_START farther than that from the plane.
  4. Score each remaining one: distance × (1 − BIAS × cos(approach − wind_from)). A landing with no next waypoint to take a heading from is scored on distance alone.
  5. Pick the lowest score. If that isn't the nearest one, the GCS shows DO_LAND_START n (upwind, nearest was m).
  6. If the cap left nothing: with LAND_WIND_STRICT 0, fall back to upstream nearest across all; with 1, refuse and keep loitering.

Things to know

  • Where the cap is measured from. With RTL_AUTOLAND 1 the choice runs after the plane reaches the RTL loiter point, so the cap is effectively measured from home. With RTL_AUTOLAND 2, a battery failsafe or a GCS DO_LAND_START it is measured from wherever the plane is when it fires.
  • The approach heading is the bearing from the DO_LAND_START point to the next waypoint in the mission. Put the first approach waypoint on the line you want to land along.
  • Without a pitot the wind estimate needs a few turns to settle and can be badly wrong (one Terrahawk flight showed 12 m/s of wind that wasn't there). A wrong estimate picks a wrong landing, so keep the bias moderate (0.3–0.5).
  • Strict mode can lose the aircraft. A battery failsafe with nothing inside the cap will loiter until the battery runs out. Leave it at 0 unless you are watching every flight.
