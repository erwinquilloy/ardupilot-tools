ArduPilot DataFlash .bin

Log Analyzer

Open a flight log to get the aircraft, firmware and date, a list of problems found in the log, the flight path on a map, controller demand versus actual, tuning parameters, efficiency and an estimated flight time for your battery, motors and props. The log is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Open a flight log to start

Choose a DataFlash .bin log from the SD card or downloaded from Mission Planner, or drop it anywhere on this page. Large logs take a few seconds.

  • What happened the flight told in order, with the moments where something went wrong
  • Warnings EKF, vibration, compass, GPS, battery, motors and failsafes
  • Map and charts flight path by mode, altitude, speed and current
  • Demand vs actual rate, attitude, TECS and altitude controllers
  • Efficiency and comparison cruise power and Wh/km, and several flights side by side with your notes
  • Flight time estimator from your battery, weight, motors and props

What happened

Each armed period told in order from the log: takeoff, modes, transitions, landing, and the problems found at the moment they happened. Lines marked Why link a problem to other data recorded at the same time. These are fixed rules, so they describe and point; they do not replace a proper diagnosis.

Flight overview

Drag across a chart to zoom. The cursor is shared with the map.

Flight path

Open home in Google Maps Hover a point on the track to point the Google Maps link at it. KML opens in Google Earth and imports into Google My Maps.

This page could not load map images from OpenStreetMap or Esri, so the track is drawn on a grid. Some embedded viewers (such as claude.ai artifacts) and some networks block them. Open the page directly at erwinquilloy.github.io/ardupilot-tools for map and satellite backgrounds, or use the Google Maps link and KML export.

Messages, modes and events

Everything the firmware wrote as text, every mode change with its reason, and logged events, in time order.

Warnings

Automatic checks on this log. Thresholds are rules of thumb; open the time to see the moment on the charts and the map.

Demand vs actual

What each controller asked for against what the aircraft did. Lag is a cross-correlation estimate over armed time; RMS and p95 are of the error while armed.

Drag across a chart to zoom in on a manoeuvre.

Compare flights

Add more logs from the same aircraft and give each one a note, such as the prop you flew (9x9, 10x8). The first log is the baseline; differences are against it. The charts above show the log you pick with View.

You can pick several files at once. Notes are kept in this browser for each file.

All parameters

Flight time and airframe estimator

A calculator. Rows tagged log are measured in this flight (hover and cruise power, current, RPM, speeds). Every other number is calculated from those and the specs you enter, so it is only as good as your specs. Nothing you enter changes the log analysis. Entries are kept in this browser.
