Telemetry widget · FrSky Ethos and EdgeTX

OmniDeck

One telemetry display for ArduPilot, INAV and Betaflight. A full-screen widget with a horizon and tapes, a moving map with optional satellite tiles, status messages, voice call-outs and a flight summary, the same on every flight controller and every radio link.

0.2.0-test31Test buildEthos · EdgeTX

What it does

  • Avionics: artificial horizon with pitch ladder and roll scale, speed and altitude tapes, heading tape with a home marker, vertical speed bar.
  • Map: vector map with range rings, flight trail, home and the next waypoint, or satellite and street tiles on the SD card. Aircraft-centred or home-centred, north up or heading up.
  • Flight data blocks: distance to home with an arrow, total distance, flight time, max airspeed and ground speed, battery gauge, mAh used, current, throttle and efficiency.
  • Status messages from ArduPilot, with severity colours and a beep per message.
  • Ready to arm while disarmed, and voice call-outs for flight modes, arming, battery, failsafes and GPS.
  • Flight summary on disarm: flight time, distance, furthest from home, max altitude and speeds, energy used, lowest voltage and link quality.
  • Missions: ArduPilot waypoint files or waypoints learned from telemetry; INAV missions downloaded over the telemetry link.

Radios

OmniDeck runs on FrSky Ethos and on EdgeTX colour radios. Both use the same screens and telemetry code; EdgeTX gets a lighter drawing mode because it stops a widget that uses too much processor time.

Flight controllers

ArduPilot

Plane, including QuadPlane, read through FrSky passthrough. Copter flight mode names are included.

  • Attitude, GPS, home distance and bearing, battery and capacity, flight mode, armed and flying flags, failsafes, waypoint, wind.
  • Status messages, shown and spoken.
  • Flight time counts only while ArduPilot reports it is flying, so time armed on the ground is left out.
  • Ready to arm is worked out from PreArm messages and the GPS, EKF and failsafe state: ArduPilot sends no ready flag.

INAV

Read from the radio's telemetry sensors. Tested with INAV 7 and 9.

  • Attitude, GPS, altitude, speeds, battery, flight mode and ready to arm, straight from INAV.
  • Airspeed only with a pitot, real or virtual.
  • Missions are downloaded from the flight controller over the telemetry link, checked over TBS Crossfire on a TX16S MK2 (see Missions).
  • No status messages: INAV doesn't send them over these links.
  • Flight time counts from arming.

Betaflight

Read from the radio's telemetry sensors.

  • Attitude, GPS, speed, battery, flight mode, and ready to arm from the CRSF flight mode text. Over S.Port the arming state is not confirmed yet.
  • No airspeed and no status messages.
  • Flight time counts from arming.

Battery capacity

ArduPilot sends its BATT_CAPACITY. INAV and Betaflight don't, so enter the pack size under Battery mAh (INAV/BF only) for the battery bar and the battery call-outs. Both also need a working current sensor.

The screens

Tap the screen or press ENTER for the next page: Main, Map, Messages, the Flight summary once a flight has ended, and Sensor check. The wheel zooms the map and scrolls the messages. On EdgeTX, touch and keys only reach the widget in full screen.

Main page

  • Top bar: flight mode, ARMED or DISARMED, model name, radio battery, GPS fix and satellites, link quality and RSSI.
  • Horizon: speed tape on the left (airspeed when the aircraft sends it, else ground speed), with ground speed in the green box under it; altitude tape on the right.
  • Ready to arm: green READY TO ARM or red NOT READY TO ARM under the aircraft symbol while disarmed.
  • HOME: distance to home and an arrow pointing to home from the nose. The small note is the total distance flown.
  • FLIGHT TIME: white while the clock runs. After the disarm the last flight's time stays, dimmed, until the next arming.
  • MAX AS / MAX GS: highest airspeed and ground speed this flight; the bar shows the speed now against that maximum.
  • Battery: capacity left as a bar, with the voltage on top. Green, then amber below 30 %, red below 15 % or on a battery failsafe.
  • USED, CURR, THR: mAh used, current with its maximum, throttle with the live efficiency.
  • Bottom line: the latest status message.

Map, messages and summary

  • Map: + and − zoom by hand; the PLANE / HOME button switches between aircraft-centred and home-centred. Home-centred zooms out by itself to keep the aircraft on the map. An arrow on the edge points to home when it is off the map.
  • Messages: the last 60 messages, newest at the bottom, with their age. Scroll back with the wheel or by dragging; a "N newer" tag jumps back. The same message repeated shows once with a count, for example (x4).
  • Flight summary: opens about 1 second after the disarm, if the aircraft was armed for at least 10 s. Arming again goes straight back to the main page. Tap or press ENTER to leave it.
  • Sensor check: the link and flight controller detected, then each value with the sensor it comes from. It is the quickest way to see why something shows --.

On EdgeTX (TX16S MK2)

  • The same pages, drawn a little lighter: the minimap on the main page has no trail or mission (the Map page has both), and the tapes label every other step.
  • Outside full screen the widget gets no touch or keys, so the flight summary goes back to the main page by itself after 15 s.

Install

Ethos

  1. Connect the radio by USB and choose the SD card, or use a card reader.
  2. Copy the scripts and bitmaps folders from the package to the root of the card. You should have scripts/omnideck/main.lua and the other files.
  3. Updating: overwrite the .lua files in scripts/omnideck/.
  4. Eject the card and restart the radio.
  5. Add OmniDeck to a screen with a full-screen layout. In a smaller slot it shows only the minimap.
  6. Long-press the widget and choose Configure widget for the settings.

EdgeTX

  1. Connect the radio by USB and pick USB Storage (SD), or use a card reader.
  2. Copy the WIDGETS and SCRIPTS folders to the root of the card: WIDGETS/OmniDeck/ and SCRIPTS/TOOLS/OmniDeck Setup.lua, OmniDeck Check.lua.
  3. Updating: delete the .luac files in WIDGETS/OmniDeck/ first, so the radio compiles the new version.
  4. Eject the card and restart the radio.
  5. Add a screen with a one-zone layout, no top bar, sliders or trims, and pick OmniDeck for the zone.
  6. Long-press the widget and choose Full screen to change pages. Long-press RTN to leave.
  7. Settings: SYS › Tools › OmniDeck Setup.

Not in the widget list? Run SYS › Tools › OmniDeck Check: it loads the widget the way EdgeTX does and shows the exact error.

Map tiles

Without tiles you get a vector map with range rings. For satellite or street maps, OmniDeck reads the tile folders used by the ETHOS Mapping Widget, so tiles made for it, and old Yaapu Google tiles, work as they are.

  1. On a PC open the High Resolution Map Generator in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Set Output Target to b14ckyy ETHOS Mapping Widget.
  3. Pick the provider and map type, for example Google Satellite, and a zoom range such as 12 to 17. Draw a box larger than the area you fly in.
  4. Save to the root of the SD card, or extract the ZIP there. Chrome and Edge can write straight to the card.
  5. Restart the radio and pick the set under Map tiles. Only sets found on the card are listed, with their zoom range.

Missions

INAV

OmniDeck downloads the mission from the flight controller over the telemetry link (CRSF/ELRS or S.Port), a few seconds after the link comes up. Checked over TBS Crossfire on a TX16S MK2.

  • Waypoints show numbered, holds as H and landings as L; the active waypoint is green.
  • A mission uploaded after the radio started shows within about 10 s while the aircraft is disarmed, without a restart.
  • Only the first mission on the flight controller is shown. Upload it and save it to EEPROM in INAV Configurator, or the flight controller won't have it after a power cycle.
  • INAV mission download in the settings turns it off. The Sensor check page shows what INAV reported and how many waypoints arrived.

ArduPilot

ArduPilot's radio telemetry has no mission download.

  • Ethos: copy a Mission Planner / QGC .waypoints file to scripts/omnideck/missions/ and pick it under Mission file. The whole route draws, with the active waypoint in green.
  • Without a file, and on EdgeTX (which has no Mission file setting yet): each waypoint is placed on the map when it becomes active, from ArduPilot's distance and bearing to it, so the route builds up as you fly.

Betaflight has no missions.

Voice and alarms

  • Call-outs: flight modes, arm and disarm, battery percent (50, 40, 30, 25, 20, 15, 10 and 5 % while armed), failsafes, GPS fix changes, telemetry lost and recovered, ready and not ready to arm, and status messages worth hearing, such as "Takeoff complete".
  • Voice packs: Aria, Christopher and Michelle come with the package. A copy of Yaapu's English sound folder in the audio folder also works, because the file names match. Ethos: scripts/omnideck/audio/. EdgeTX: WIDGETS/OmniDeck/audio/.
  • Message beep: one blip for info, a double beep for a warning, a two-tone alarm for an error. A burst of messages gives one beep.
  • Ready to arm on ArduPilot: NOT READY TO ARM holds for 40 s after a PreArm message or any critical message, and while a fitted GPS has no 3D fix, the EKF reports a problem or a failsafe is active (no GPS at all does not block it). READY TO ARM needs 40 s disarmed with none of these. The flight controller still has the last word.

Reporting a problem

Please send the radio model and its Ethos or EdgeTX version, the flight controller and its version, the link, and a photo of the Sensor check page (page 5). For a script error, send the exact text. A short video helps for anything that moves.

  • No telemetry on INAV or Betaflight: discover the sensors again on the radio with the aircraft powered.
  • Horizon level on INAV over S.Port or F.Port: turn on Use Pitch & Roll sensors in telemetry in INAV.
  • Flight time 00:00 on ArduPilot on the bench: expected. It only counts while ArduPilot reports it is flying.
  • No flight summary: the aircraft has to be armed for at least 10 s.
