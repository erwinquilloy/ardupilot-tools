      OmniDeck Mono

      OmniDeck for EdgeTX radios with a 128 × 64 black-and-white screen. A free telemetry script for ArduPilot, INAV and Betaflight: horizon, map, messages, flight summary, flight log and voice call-outs, on six small pages.

      Questions, ideas and flight reports: chat with us on the Arduplane Stratos Discord. The colour version is the OmniDeck guide.

      0.1.1 betaTX12 MK2 · GX12 · Pocket · BoxerEdgeTX 2.12ELRS · TBS · mLRS · S.Port · F.PortGPL-3.0

      Install

      1. Download OmniDeck_Mono_0.1.1.zip from the OmniDeck Mono release.
      2. Connect the radio by USB and pick USB Storage (SD), or put the card in a card reader.
      3. Copy the SCRIPTS folder from the zip to the root of the card, merging with the folder already there: 
        SCRIPTS/
├── TELEMETRY/omnid.luac        the telemetry script
├── OMNID/
│   ├── telem.luac etx.luac pmain.luac …   compiled pages and decoders
│   ├── audio/                  voice packs: Aria, Guy
│   ├── tones/                  info, warn, crit beeps
│   └── logs/                   one text file per flight, if turned on
└── TOOLS/
    └── OmniDeck Setup.lua      settings, saved per model
      4. On the model: MODEL › DISPLAY, pick a screen, set it to Script and choose omnid.
      5. Open it from the main screen with the radio's telemetry key (TELE on the TX12 MK2).
      6. Optional: run SYS › Tools › OmniDeck Setup once per model to pick units, voice and alarms.
      Keep the .luac files. The telemetry code ships compiled because the radio runs out of memory compiling it itself ("not enough memory"). Don't put telem.lua on the card.
      Updating. Copy the new SCRIPTS folder over the old one and replace the files. Your per-model settings in SCRIPTS/OMNID/<model>.cfg stay.

      Main page

      • Top bar: flight mode; radio battery; ARM or DIS in a block; GPS fix and satellites (3D14).
      • Heading box at the top, with the home marker H under it. The marker slides with the bearing to home and stops at the edge with a < or > arrow when home is too far off the nose.
      • Speed box on the left: airspeed when a sensor is fitted, with a GS box under it; ground speed alone without one.
      • Altitude box on the right edge of the horizon, and the pitch ladder with its numbers on the left.
      • Right column: seven labelled rows, label on the left and value right-aligned.
      RowShowsNotes
      ALTaltitude above home120m; from 1000 m 1.00km to 9.99km, then 10.0km; blinks while under the LOW ALT alarm
      SPDairspeed, or ground speed without a sensorin the Setup speed unit
      Hdistance to home; the arrow right after the H points home999m, 1.5k, 25.0k
      BATflight battery voltagethe value inverts when low and blinks when critical (Setup voltage alarms)
      CURcurrent12.0A; whole amps from 100 A
      EFlive efficiency, small font2.9WH/km; hidden below 2 m/s ground speed
      TIMflight time, small fonton ArduPilot it counts only while ArduPilot reports it is flying

      The column is 54 pixels wide: nine characters, or eleven in the small font. When a label and its value don't fit side by side with a space, the label is left out and the value stays, as for altitude in km and the longer mAh efficiency units.

      Other pages

      ENTER steps through them in this order. Messages appear only with ArduPilot. Flight stats and Sensor check are hidden until you turn them on in Setup.

      Map

      Setup MAP VIEW picks what stays in the centre, as on the colour builds.

      • Aircraft-centred (default): the aircraft stays in the middle. The wheel (or + / −) zooms: right closer, left further out, through range steps from 50 m to 50 km from the centre to the nearest edge. It starts at 300 m and keeps its zoom when you change pages.
      • Home-centred: home stays in the middle and the map zooms itself: out at once when the aircraft nears the edge, back in one step at a time (at most every 2 s) once it is well inside, and to the closest step again each time you arm.
      • Home is a boxed H; when it is off the map it stays at the edge, so it shows the way home. Trail dots, the active waypoint (ArduPilot sends only that one) with a dotted line to it, a scale bar at the bottom left and N at the top right.
      • Coordinates along the bottom: the aircraft's latitude and longitude to 5 decimals (about 1 m; the radio's Lua numbers are 32-bit). With no GPS fix the last known position shows, marked LAST, to help find a plane that is down out of sight.

      Flight summary and log

      The flight summary opens by itself at disarm when the aircraft was armed for at least 10 seconds: flight time, distance flown (the whole track), furthest from home, max altitude, max airspeed and ground speed, efficiency and the lowest voltage. It goes back to the start page by itself after 15 seconds; ENTER goes back sooner.

      With FLIGHT LOG on in Setup, the same flights are also written as text files to SCRIPTS/OMNID/logs/. Set the radio's clock, or the file names and dates will be wrong.

      Voice and alarms

      The same call-outs as the colour builds, from the packs in SCRIPTS/OMNID/audio/ (Aria and Guy are included): flight modes, arm and disarm, battery percent while armed (50, 40, 30, 25, 20, 15, 10 and 5 %), failsafes, GPS fix changes, telemetry lost and recovered, ready and not ready to arm, ArduPilot messages worth hearing such as "Takeoff complete", a beep for other messages, the low and critical voltage per cell when set in Setup, and the low-altitude "Terrain, pull up" when LOW ALT is set: only while armed and once the aircraft has been above the setting, so the take-off and a landing approach from below don't count; again every 15 s while low, with the ALT value blinking on the main page. "OmniDeck telemetry ready" plays when the radio starts or you select the model.

      ArduPilot parameters

      OmniDeck Mono has no parameter editor of its own. To read and change flight controller settings from the radio, install the OmniDeck Param Editor (was AP Param Editor) and open it from SYS › Tools › OmniDeck Param Editor. It covers ArduPilot, INAV 9.1 or later and Betaflight 4.5 / 2025.12 / 2026.6, and runs on black-and-white and colour EdgeTX radios and on Ethos.

      LinkOmniDeck Param Editor needs (ArduPilot)
      S.Port, F.PortArduPilot with FrSky passthrough telemetry
      ExpressLRS, TBS CrossfireArduPlane Stratos v2.2.1 or later on the flight controller
      mLRSTx Ser Port = cbridge on the mLRS transmitter; any ArduPilot

      While it runs, EdgeTX pauses the OmniDeck screen. While armed the editor is view only: turn on AIR EDITS in its tab list to save any parameter while armed (a change takes effect at once, so use small steps). AIR EDITS is OFF again every time the editor opens.

      Memory

      A TX12 MK2 gives Lua scripts about 127 KB. EdgeTX stops every Lua script with "Lua disabled!" once that is used up, and its count includes garbage not yet collected. OmniDeck Mono loads one page at a time, clears the garbage once it is 6 KB above the live data, and never lets the total pass 106 KB.

      Measured on a TX12 MK2 with 0.1.0Lua memory
      Telemetry script, ArduPilot or INAV, flying pagesabout 97 of 127 KB
      At disarm with the flight log on101 of 127 KB
      Over mLRS, which sends bigger telemetry frames107 of 127 KB
      Measured on a TX12 MK2 with 0.1.1Live / peak
      ELRS, armed over 30 min96 / 106 of 127 KB

      0.1.1 leaves out the colour builds' code that Mono never uses and loads the voice phrase tables only when a mode changes or a message arrives: on the TX12 MK2 that took the live data from 104 to 93 KB and the peak from 114 to 100 KB (ELRS).

      The Link check page shows LUA MEM as two figures: the live data, and PK, the highest it has reached, garbage included.

      Problems

      You seeDo this
      "not enough memory" when the script startsA telem.lua source file is on the card. Delete it and keep telem.luac.
      "Syntax error" over the page (for example "bad argument #1 to 'getValue'"), while OmniDeck runs fine from the file browserAnother telemetry script on one of the model's DISPLAY screens is failing, such as EdgeTX's stock gplusl.lua on a model with no GPS sensor. EdgeTX runs every screen's script in the background and shows any script's error over the page you are on. Set that screen to None or remove the script. If OmniDeck itself can't start, it says so: OmniDeck: not started, with the reason and LUA MEM, and it tries again every 5 s.
      No telemetry on INAV or BetaflightDiscover the sensors again on the radio with the aircraft powered.
      Horizon stays level on INAV over S.Port or F.PortTurn on Use Pitch & Roll sensors in telemetry in INAV.
      Flight time 00:00 on ArduPilot on the benchExpected; it counts only while ArduPilot reports it is flying.
      No flight summaryThe aircraft has to be armed for at least 10 seconds, and SUMMARY must be On disarm.
      Setup changes don't showLeave the tool with RTN; settings apply when the telemetry screen starts again.
      Log file dates are wrongSet the radio's clock.
      mLRS Configurator stays on "parameters loading"The model has a second module set to CRSF. Set the RF section that is not mLRS to OFF.
      ExpressLRS script stays on "External TX…"The model's internal module is not set to CRSF. Set Internal RF to CRSF and External RF to OFF.

      Report anything else on the Arduplane Stratos Discord with the radio and EdgeTX version, the flight controller and firmware, the link, and a photo of the Link check or Sensor check page.

      Compared with colour OmniDeck

      • No map tiles. The map shows only the active waypoint, because ArduPilot's passthrough sends only that one. No mission files or INAV mission download.
      • No touch, speed tapes or side panel: the screen is 128 × 64.
      • No built-in parameter editor; use the OmniDeck Param Editor.
